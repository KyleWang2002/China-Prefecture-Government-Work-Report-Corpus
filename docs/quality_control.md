# Quality control

Quality control was integrated into collection rather than postponed to the end.

## Checks used in practice

### Document identity
- Is this actually a municipal government work report rather than a people's congress report, CPPCC report, plan report, or excerpt?
- Does the title / opening identify the municipal government and the correct annual session?
- Does the report year agree with the meeting date?

### Completeness
- Extremely short text is flagged for inspection.
- A file that ends abruptly or contains only highlights / excerpts is not treated as equivalent to a full report.
- When a municipal website provides an abridged version, other archival copies are compared where possible.

### OCR and layout
- Multi-column pages are checked for reading-order errors.
- Poor scans are reviewed for dropped numerals, confused characters, and broken punctuation.
- Page numbers, yearbook headers, watermarks, and unrelated adjacent material are removed conservatively.

### Duplicate and year checks
- Similar texts in neighboring years are compared when duplication is suspected.
- Year labels are checked against the meeting date and internal references to previous/current-year work.

### Administrative geography
- City-year absence is interpreted together with the administrative-change table.
- A city that did not yet exist in its current prefecture-level form is not mechanically coded as a missing report.

## What `verified` means

`verified = yes` means the city-level series has completed the project's current manual coverage check.

It does **not** mean every character in every historical OCR file is guaranteed error-free. Residual OCR errors can remain, especially in older scans, and those are corrected as they are found.
