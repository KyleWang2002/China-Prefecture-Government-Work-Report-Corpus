# Research context

Government work reports are already used as empirical text in economics, political economy, finance, and related fields. This project does not claim that the document type or text-analysis approach is new.

A few examples:

1. **Jiaxuan Lu (2023), Journal of Law, Economics, and Organization**  
   *Leadership Rotations and the Convergence of Subnational Economic Policies in China: Evidence from Provincial Government Work Reports*  
   https://doi.org/10.1093/jleo/ewac026

2. **Yongze Yu, Ke Li, Shenglan Duan, and Chenchen Song (2023), Energy Economics**  
   *Economic growth and environmental pollution in China: New evidence from government work reports*  
   Uses city government work reports from 2004-2019 to study growth targets and pollution.  
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eneco.2023.106803

3. **Zheng Lichen, Dai Hongwei, and Wang Jianing (2024), 财经研究 / Journal of Finance and Economics**  
   *产业政策优化、地方经济竞争与官员的“理性选择”——基于地级市政府工作报告的实证分析*  
   Uses 2008-2018 reports from 254 prefecture-level cities.  
   https://doi.org/10.16538/j.cnki.jfe.20240219.301

4. **Finance Research Letters (2025)**  
   *Government disclosure specificity and stock price synchronicity: Evidence from local government work reports in China*  
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2024.106463

The motivation for this repository is upstream of these applications: longer and cleaner city series require archival recovery, layout-aware document processing, and careful treatment of administrative changes.
